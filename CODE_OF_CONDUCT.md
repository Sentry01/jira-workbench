# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a
problem, or propose a change without fear of being belittled for it.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that make this project better:

- Assuming good intent, and asking before assuming incompetence.
- Giving feedback on the work, with a reason and a suggested alternative.
- Accepting that a maintainer may say no, and that "not now" is a valid answer.
- Remembering that the person on the other end is doing this in their spare time.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions and
commit history) and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Sentry01/jira-workbench/security/advisories/new)
(it is the most private reporting channel available on the repository) or by
contacting the maintainer directly through GitHub.

Reports are reviewed as promptly as a personal project allows. The maintainer will
respect the privacy of the reporter and may take any action deemed appropriate,
including warning, temporary blocking, or a permanent ban from project spaces.
